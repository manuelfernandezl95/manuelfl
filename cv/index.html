    • Co-supervising a Ph.D. student (ongoing, through weekly group meetings) on inhomogeneous superconductivity in layered cuprates and nickelates, using extended Gutzwiller approximations complemented with AI-trained variational and numerical methods such as Monte Carlo; a collaborative manuscript is currently in its final stage.
  • 2019 - 2024

    Madrid, Spain

    Researcher Formation Fellow (FPI)
    Autonomous University of Madrid (UAM)
    • Implemented a finite-T slave-rotor mean-field framework for topological Mott transitions; benchmarked local limits against DMFT.
    • Identified flat-band topological superconductivity and localized Majorana edge modes in metal-organic frameworks (Phys. Rev. B 106, 235129, 2022).
    • Performed slab Green’s-function calculations revealing spinon Fermi arcs, a novel type of topological surface state (Phys. Rev. B 109, 085137, 2024).
    • Derived effective Hubbard models from first-principles DFT band structures for strongly correlated-material modeling (Phys. Rev. B 111, 165133, 2025).
    • Research project: New phenomena and applications of topological strongly correlated materials in and out of equilibrium (Ref. RTI2018-098452-B-I00; IP: Jaime Merino Troncoso, FPI PRE2019-087603) — supervised a master’s student on code adaptation for correlated nodal-line semimetals, providing the first theoretical realization of the observed pressure-induced Mott transition; developed a semi-analytical technique extended to finite temperature revealing that non-local correlations lead to spinon Fermi arcs, with predicted ARPES signatures on pyrochlore iridates; investigated correlated topology in flat-band decorated honeycomb lattices motivated by MOFs, with exact diagonalization revealing a flat-band quantum spin liquid consistent with experiments; and explored flat-band unconventional superconductivity from doping this spin liquid, characterizing localized Majorana edge modes and their potential for topological quantum computing.
    • Lecturer & Lab Supervisor, Physics Laboratory course for Environmental Science undergraduates, 2nd year (2021–2022).
